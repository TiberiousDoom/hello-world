    How to play
    
        
      
          
    • Start with wood: with no tool in hand, tap a tree to chop it, or ore and stone to mine them, and carry them to the hub. Research Forestry for lumber camps and sawmills.
      • 
          
    • Industry brings extractors over ore and furnaces to smelt it; then run belts to workshops and the hub.
      • 
          
    • Machines take input from any side and output from the middle of the side their arrow marks.
      • 
          
    • Buildings and research are paid for from the hub's stock, so keep it supplied. Select the hub to open Research.
      • 
          
    • Splitters take items from behind and send them out the other three sides in turn. Underground belts pass items under anything: place an entrance, then an exit ahead of it.
      • 
          
    • Bugs live in territories away from the hub, marked by a nest mound. They leave you alone unless provoked: by building in or next to their territory, by a building coming close to one of them, or by shooting them. Then they attack your nearest buildings until things calm down.
      • 
          
    • Turrets fire iron plates and cannons fire gears. Feed them by belt, from the hub with a hub port, or place them touching the hub.
      • 
          
    • Research the Shipyard, build the ship stage by stage, and launch it to escape. The engines wake every nest nearby.
      • 
        
    
        
    Controls
    
        
      
          
    • Desktop: click to build or select. Drag out a line of belts or walls, then press ✓ or Enter to build it (⟳ or R reverses or rotates it, ✕ or Esc cancels). Right-click to put down the tool, right-drag or WASD to pan. The wheel rotates while you're placing a building and zooms otherwise (Ctrl+wheel always zooms). R also rotates, X demolishes, 1–9 picks buildings, Space pauses and Esc cancels.
      • 
          
    • Touch: tap to place a blueprint, then ✓ to build it (or tap the same spot again). Drag with a tool to lay a line, drag with no tool to pan, pinch to zoom. Menu → "Confirm before building" switches blueprints off.
      • 
          
    • Playing together: press P (or hold a finger still on empty ground) to point at a spot for the others. Only the host pauses or changes the speed.
