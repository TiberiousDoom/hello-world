How to play

  • Put extractors on resource nodes and run belts to furnaces, workshops and the hub.
  • Machines take input from any side and output where their arrow points.
  • Buildings and research are paid for from the hub's stock, so keep it supplied.
  • Splitters take items from behind and send them out the other three sides in turn. Underground belts pass items under anything: place an entrance, then an exit ahead of it.
  • You owe the Company. Meet its shipping contracts (in your inbox) to earn wages. Miss them and you're fined. The Company Store sells emergency supplies on credit.
  • Once A Friend gets in touch, the black market (the eye at the top) opens. A Stash hides items from the Company, and a Skimmer diverts some from your belts. Keep contraband away from the hub: if suspicion gets high, an audit drone sweeps the area around it.
  • Enemies attack in waves. Turrets fire iron plates and cannons fire gears. Feed them by belt, or from the hub with a hub port.

Controls

  • Desktop: click to build or select. Drag out a line of belts or walls, then press ✓ or Enter to build it (⟳ or R reverses or rotates it, ✕ or Esc cancels). Right-click to put down the tool, right-drag or WASD to pan. The wheel rotates while you're placing a building and zooms otherwise (Ctrl+wheel always zooms). R also rotates, X demolishes, 1–9 picks buildings, Space pauses and Esc cancels.
  • Touch: tap to place a blueprint, then ✓ to build it (or tap the same spot again). Drag with a tool to lay a line, drag with no tool to pan, pinch to zoom. Menu → "Confirm before building" switches blueprints off.
